# 용어집 — Korean game vocabulary

One word per idea, everywhere. Struct-type data (classes, weapons, abilities)
is in `runtime/terms.json`; this page is for everything else and agrees
with it.

## Voice

- Players are strategy gamers, not crypto users. The chain stays out of
  sight: no 블록체인, 지갑, 토큰, 트랜잭션, 가스, 서명 where a game word does
  the job (see the table). Webapp's English is already mostly game words;
  keep it that way in Korean.
- Narration, help text, system messages: 합니다체 (…합니다, …하세요).
- HR Bot and corporate callers: polite, a little stiff and cheerful.
- Buttons, tabs, headers: short nouns or noun+하기 (확인, 취소, 배치, 공격).
- After a `${name}` whose last sound is unknown, use a particle that fits
  both (`(이)가`, `(을)를`, `(으)로`) or rephrase so none is needed.
- Numbers, units and ids stay as they are: `W`, `kW`, `g`, `PID`, `#`, `1-42`.
